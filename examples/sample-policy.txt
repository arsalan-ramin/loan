Orchid Project — Document Review Policy

This policy describes the approved document review process for the Orchid project.
Every submission must identify its purpose, responsible team, effective date, and
supporting evidence. The operations team checks each document before publishing.

Effective date: 20 October 2026
Responsible team: Document Operations
Status: Approved

Reviewers should confirm that all required information is available, record any
missing details, and retain the final decision report with the original submission.
Automated rule results support the reviewer but do not replace professional judgment.
Questions about the policy should be directed to the responsible team for clarification.
